## 6. The three known bugs

### 6.1 Pilot gate printing GOOD at a 26% failure rate — **FIXED**

`run_eval.py:412–447`. The band is now two named constants (`FAILURE_RATE_FLOOR = 0.20`,
`FAILURE_RATE_CEILING = 0.55`), the gate prints the band it is testing against, and it
reports a 95% CI with the rule-of-three correction at 0 and 1. It refuses to rule when
the interval straddles a boundary. Verified live:

- mock wide, n=51: 27.5%, CI [15%, 40%] → `UNRESOLVED`
- mock claude, n=51: 23.5%, CI [12%, 35%] → `UNRESOLVED`
- mock claude probe, n=100: 29.0%, CI [20%, 38%] → `IN BAND`
- replay of the real run, n=4: 0.0%, CI [0%, 75%] → `UNRESOLVED`

The uncommitted diff adds a per-domain breakdown and a failure-rate-by-difficulty-band
table. On the n=100 probe both domains individually read `unresolved` even though the
aggregate reads `IN BAND` — which is exactly the split verdict the aggregate would have
hidden.

### 6.2 The oracle not bounding the cascade — **FIXED**

`policies.policy_oracle` (policies.py:943) now enumerates the full reachable action
space in cost order: one greedy answer per rung of `models.TIERS`, plus majority-of-k
over cheap samples on math, which is the action the old oracle was missing and the
math cascade could reach. It charges the cheapest correct action, or the cheapest
action overall when nothing is correct — so it is both an accuracy ceiling and a cost
floor.

`run_eval.oracle_bound_check` (run_eval.py:277) asserts and prints the invariant every
run. Verified `ok` on all three domains across every ladder and mode I ran.

### 6.3 The mock inflating self-consistency — **STILL LIVE, now documented**

Not fixed, and cannot be fixed without changing what the mock is. `models._wrong_answer`
scatters wrong answers across distinct values, so majority-of-5 recovers the truth
whenever 2 of 5 samples are right. Real models cluster on the *same* wrong answer.

I measured the size of it directly on the 60 math tasks, `wide` cheap rung:

```
greedy correct 41, greedy wrong 19
majority-of-5 RESCUED 16/19 wrong greedy answers (84%)
majority-of-5 BROKE   0/41 correct greedy answers (0%)
net: 68.3% -> 95.0%   (+26.7 points)
```

Published self-consistency gains are low single digits. **This is the largest single
fabricated effect in the repo**, it is what drives math cascade accuracy to 100%, and
it propagates into the cascade's routing skill of 100% and into the AUC advantage.
`NOTES.md` §3 and `STATUS.md` §5 both name it and say not to quote the mock figure,
which is the right handling short of a real run.

### 7.2 The routing-skill table rests on a single coin flip — **highest analytical severity**

`random_matched` is the null hypothesis and the denominator of every routing-skill
figure. `policies.RANDOM_SEED = 0` picks one draw, and `run_eval.routing_skill` reads
that one draw. `random_baseline.py`, which reported mean/sd/p5/p95 over 200 seeds and
P(random ≥ predictive), was deleted in `344fe06`. The comment at policies.py:522 says
`frontier.py`'s rate sweep "replaced a dedicated seed-sweep script" — but sweeping the
*rate* across its range and averaging over *draws at the matched rate* are different
operations, and only the second one quantifies the sampling noise in the baseline the
skill table divides by.

The cost is measurable. Recomputing over 200 seeds on the mock wide eval split:

```
predictive        : acc 88.2%  cost/task 0.001396  expensive-rate 37.3%
random_matched, 200 seeds:
  acc            mean 82.0%  sd 3.0%   p5 78.4%   p95 86.3%
  cost/task      mean 0.001338  sd 0.000214
  expensive-rate mean 36.7%  sd 6.1%   p5 27.5%   p95 47.1%
  P(random_matched acc >= predictive acc) = 3.5%

seed 0, the draw actually reported: acc 80.4%  cost 0.001148  rate 31.4%
  -> spends 18% LESS than predictive; 20th percentile on cost, 24th on accuracy
```

Two consequences:

1. **The "cost-matched" baseline is not cost-matched in the reported draw.** On code
   specifically, seed 0 routes 3 of 21 tasks expensive (14%) against `predictive`'s 7
   of 21 (33%) — $0.000568 vs $0.001296, **56% less spend**. The rate is matched in
   expectation at calibration time; the realisation at n=21 drifts nearly 2 sd.

2. **The printed skill figures are off by 22 to 44 points:**

   | domain | predictive | oracle | rand (seed 0) | skill printed | rand (200-seed mean) | skill corrected |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | all | 88.2% | 98.0% | 80.4% | 44.4% | 82.0% | **38.8%** |

### 7.5 `cascade_routing` is dominated on two of three ladders
